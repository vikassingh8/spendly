Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Spendly collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address and password when you register. Passwords are stored only in hashed form, never in plain text.
  • Expense data: the amounts, categories, dates and descriptions you enter.
  • Technical data: basic information such as browser type and session cookies needed to keep you signed in.

2. How we use your information

  • to create and maintain your account;
  • to store your expenses and show you summaries and reports;
  • to keep you signed in and secure your account;
  • to improve and fix problems with the service.

3. What we do not do

We do not sell your personal or financial data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

4. Cookies

Spendly uses a session cookie to remember that you are signed in. It is essential for the service to work and is not used for tracking or advertising.

5. Data storage and security

Your data is stored in a database on our servers. We take reasonable measures to protect it, including hashing passwords and limiting access. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data and expense records are removed.

7. Third-party services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser may contact Google's servers when you visit the site. We do not otherwise share your information with third parties, except where required by law.

8. Your rights

You can view and edit your expense data within the app. You may also ask us to correct, export or delete your personal information at any time by contacting us.

9. Children's privacy

Spendly is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest revision. See also our Terms and Conditions.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us at privacy@spendly.example.
